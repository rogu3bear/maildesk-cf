# Marketing

## Target Market & Avatar

**Niche:** technical operators running shared role mail on Cloudflare.

**Avatar:** a founder-operator or platform engineer who needs domain-consistent inbound routing and replies without handing product truth to a personal mailbox or trusting undocumented dashboard state.

**USP:** A Cloudflare-native mail desk where Rust policy decides the route, operators see the identity story, and cfctl proves the infrastructure state.

**Primary goal:** Open the desk.

## Before / During / After Grid

| Phase | Square | Current state | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before | Discovery | Shared role mail feels distributed across inbox rules and Cloudflare resources | Explain the chain of custody in one screen |
| Before | Evaluation | Trust hinges on identity, persistence, and deployment proof | Show architecture, readiness vocabulary, and security boundaries |
| During | First use | Operator needs to know whether the desk is real or sample data | Use honest preview/empty/loading states and evidence labels |
| During | Core use | Operator triages, reads, and replies | Keep attention, route, identity, and audit in one workflow |
| After | Verification | A queued or deployed state may be mistaken for complete | Keep delivery and readiness planes distinct |
